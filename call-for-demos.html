2nd ESR Workshop · NeurIPS 2026

Call for Demos

A dedicated track for novel, interactive, and visionary systems: embodied agents you can watch act, and world models you can watch obey physics.

Deadline Sep 5, 2026 Any format Non-archival

Scope

What we are looking for

Where the paper track emphasizes rigorous evaluation, the demo track emphasizes systems that work, and systems that reveal where the frontier actually is.

We invite interactive embodied systems, real-world agent behaviors, and physically-grounded world models. A strong demo shows something a reader cannot get from a table of numbers: how an agent behaves when the world does not cooperate, or where a world model's physics quietly breaks down. Demos that illustrate current limitations as honestly as they illustrate progress are welcome.

The demo track shares the same reviewing process and the same deadlines as the paper track.

Two tracks

Demo tracks

Active embodied agents

Embodied agents executing complex tasks that illustrate both the frontiers and the current limitations of real-world embodied spatial intelligence.

  • Object manipulation
  • Dynamic navigation
  • Problem solving
  • Long-horizon planning

Physically-grounded world models

Interactive simulations or generative models that demonstrate strict adherence to physical laws, turning video generators into reliable, physics-compliant world models.

  • Strict physical-law adherence
  • Complex motions
  • Spatial memory
  • Physics-compliant video models

Guidelines

Submission guidelines

  • Format Video, PDF, a live demo link: whatever best presents your work.
  • Publication This work will be non-archival.
  • Review Demo submissions share the same reviewing process and deadlines as the paper track.
  • Presentation Accepted demos may be presented in person at the workshop or remotely.
